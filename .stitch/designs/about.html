GOLF WAS TOO PEACEFUL.

Established in 2019 by a collective of disgruntled ex-special ops and pro-golfers, SBGL was born from a single directive: disrupt the silence. We removed the etiquette, added ballistic-grade equipment, and legalized tactical interference. This isn't just a game of strokes; it's a battle for territorial dominance where the hazard is as likely to be a proximity mine as it is a sand trap.

ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOLS

01

STRICT SCORING

Standard golf scoring applies. Lowest stroke count wins the hole. Every blast, dodge, and shot counts.

02

BATTLE CREDITS

Combat gear is purchased via Battle Credits (BC). Earn BC through birdies, eagles, and tactical takedowns.

03

MINE RADIUS

Proximity mines must be deployed at least 10+ yards from any active tee box. Safety first, chaos second.

04

SMOKE OPS

Smoke grenades require active Ref approval. Unauthorized usage results in a 2-stroke tactical penalty.

05

LEGAL SABOTAGE

Sabotage of equipment/environment is fully legal. Direct physical assault is strictly prohibited.

06

BLAST RATED

All players must use SBGL-certified blast-rated golf bags. Normal canvas bags will not survive the course.

07

SUDDEN DEATH

Tie-breakers are resolved via Sudden Death Elimination on the 19th bunker. One ball, one shot, maximum hazards enabled.
