The SBGL Sponsors & Partners page — showcasing league partners, corporate sponsors, and official equipment/gear suppliers. Presented like a premium sports league sponsorship deck with tiered partner levels and brand highlights.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
Dark, aggressive sports website with military-meets-golf aesthetic.

- Platform: Web, Desktop-first (responsive)
- Palette: Bunker Black (#0d0e10) background, Battle Green (#9cff93 / #00fc40) primary, Explosion Orange (#ff7524) secondary, Snow White (#fdfbfe) text
- Typography: Space Grotesk for headers (all-caps, tracked, italic), Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for stats/numbers
- Styles: Sharp corners (minimal radius), subtle neon glow effects, high contrast dark surfaces
- Atmosphere: Elite sports sponsorship — premium, powerful, revenue-generating

**Page Structure:**
1. **Hero Section:** "LEAGUE PARTNERS" headline with tagline "Powering the World's Most Dangerous Sport." Stat badges: $42M Total Sponsorship | 18 Official Partners | 7 Title Sponsors.
2. **Tier Showcase:** 3 sponsor tiers as distinct sections — Title Sponsors (large logos, orange accent border), Premier Partners (medium logos, green accent), Official Suppliers (smaller logo grid). Each tier has a label badge and brief description.
3. **Featured Partner Spotlight:** 1 highlighted "Title Sponsor of the Season" card — large logo placeholder, partner description, quote from their rep, and CTA "View Partnership."
4. **Partner Benefits Grid:** 3-column grid showing what sponsors get — Broadcast Visibility, Course Naming Rights, Athlete Endorsement, Digital Reach, VIP Event Access, Branded Munitions. Each as a card with icon and short description.
5. **Become a Partner CTA:** Full-width dark section with "JOIN THE BATTLEFIELD" headline, brief pitch copy, and a "Request Partnership Deck" button (orange accent).
6. **Partner Logos Footer Strip:** Horizontal scrolling strip of all partner logos (placeholder boxes with brand names).
