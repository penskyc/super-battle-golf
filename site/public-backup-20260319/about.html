Origin Story

Born from
Battle

Super Battle Golf was developed in 2019 by the World Battle Sports Authority (WBSA) as a fusion of competitive stroke-play golf and tactical battlefield simulation. The sport was initially tested in controlled military training facilities before being opened to elite civilian athletes in 2020.

The first official SBGL season launched in 2020 with 12 athletes. Today, Season 7 features 48 certified competitors from 22 countries battling across 16 internationally sanctioned Battle Courses.

Regulations

Official Rules
& Regulations

01

Standard Golf Rules Apply

All USGA Rules of Golf are in effect unless directly superseded by SBGL Battle Regulations. This includes stroke count, out-of-bounds, water hazards, and green regulations. Scorecards must be signed and submitted after each round.

02

Battle Budget System

Each player receives 500 Battle Credits (BC) per round to spend on approved tactical items from the SBGL Arsenal Catalog. Unused credits carry over between holes but reset each round. No BC may be traded or gifted between players.

03

Deployment Zones

Tactical items may only be deployed within your designated Deployment Zone (DZ), which extends 10 yards in any direction from your current ball position. Deploying outside your DZ results in a 2-stroke penalty and item confiscation.

04

Direct Contact Prohibited

Physical contact between athletes is strictly forbidden. All interference must be executed via approved tactical items. Violations result in immediate disqualification from the hole and a 3-stroke penalty applied to the overall round score.

05

Ball Displacement Rules

If a tactical item causes an opponent's ball to move, the affected player may place the ball at the original position (verified by hole sensors) or play from where it lands. A free drop within one club length of the blast radius boundary is permitted.

06

Safety Gear Mandatory

All competitors must wear SBGL-certified blast armor (Mk-7 or approved equivalent), full visor helmet, and blast-resistant gloves at all times during active play. Removal of safety gear results in immediate removal from the course and a match forfeit.

07

Proximity Mine Limit

Effective Season 7: Maximum 2 proximity mines per player per round. Mines must be visually marked with the player's assigned color flag. Course marshals conduct random mine sweeps at holes 6, 12, and 18.

Safety First

Player &
Spectator Safety

Safety is the SBGL's highest priority. Every aspect of the sport is governed by the SBGL Safety Council, a body of 9 engineers, medical professionals, and former athletes.

All events feature on-site medical teams, blast containment infrastructure, and a real-time course monitoring system that can halt play within 4 seconds of detecting an unsafe detonation.
