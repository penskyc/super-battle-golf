Kowalski Shatters Volcano Ridge Record With Stunning -18 Round

In a performance that left commentators speechless, "The Crusher" Aleksander Kowalski navigated the notoriously brutal Volcano Ridge course in an unprecedented -18, using only 3 grenades and not deploying a single proximity mine. The old record of -15 had stood since Season 3.

"I've never seen anything like it," said color commentator and former Battle Golfer Dmitri Volkov. "He played the back 9 without spending a single Battle Credit. Pure golf. Pure destruction."
