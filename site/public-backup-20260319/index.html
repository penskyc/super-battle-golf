The Sport

Golf. But Deadlier.

Super Battle Golf is a full-contact sport where professional golfers compete using standard golf equipment — plus a regulated arsenal of tactical tools to hinder opponents and control the course.

💥

Battle Mechanics

Players are allocated a Battle Budget each round to purchase grenades, proximity mines, turret deployments, and shields. Strategy is everything.

⛳

Real Golf Rules

Standard USGA golf rules apply except where superseded by SBGL Battle Regulations. Handicaps, stroke play, and match play formats are all supported.

🏆

Global Competition

48 certified Battle Golfers from 22 countries compete across 16 official Battle Courses each season, culminating in the Grand Slam Championship.

🛡️

Safety Gear Required

All competitors must wear SBGL-certified blast armor, helmet, and visor. Course spectators are protected behind certified blast barriers.

🗺️

Battle Courses

Official courses feature craters, active lava zones, wind cannons, and magnetic rough. Each course has a unique Battle Hazard Rating (BHR).

📡

Live Coverage

All SBGL events broadcast live on BattleSports Network and streaming on SBGL+. 12 cameras per hole capture every shot and detonation.
